How Offices Use This Tool

Designed so individual Sub Offices (S.O), Head Offices (H.O), or the Divisional Office (DO) can maintain independent registers or consolidate records.

  1. Select Office Workspace: Choose All Offices (Shared Register) or pick a specific office in Current Office Workspace to keep each office's data isolated in browser storage.
  2. Enter & Classify Assets: Select Office / SO Name, Type of the Asset, and Category (including office furniture items such as tables, chairs, and almirahs) from standardized dropdowns.
  3. Generate Criteria Reports: Open Criteria Reports & Summary to filter by Office Name, Type of Asset, and Category in any combination.
  4. Export & Archive: Export .CSV for spreadsheet submission or .JSON backup to share/restore across systems.

Sharing & Backup Best Practices

Because this runs directly in the browser with no external server dependency, each computer stores data locally under its selected workspace key.

  • Use Export Office Backup (.JSON) before clearing browsers or transferring a register to another machine.
  • Use Import Backup (.JSON) to load an office's saved JSON snapshot into any office workspace.
  • Use Export CSV when submitting consolidated tables or importing into Excel/LibreOffice Calc.
